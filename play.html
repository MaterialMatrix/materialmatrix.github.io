Quick start

Build a profitable print farm

  1. Find customers. New shops receive few organic leads. Run an ad campaign to build demand.
  2. Build your reputation. On-time work earns trust, better leads, repeat customers, and premium campaigns.
  3. Match the material. Every order needs a compatible printer and the correct filament.
  4. Respect deadlines. Late deliveries pay 45% less and cost reputation.
  5. Schedule maintenance. Service between jobs for less money and downtime. Below 50% health, power use rises; below 35%, print speed also falls.
  6. Keep a cash reserve. Rent and electricity are collected every 30 days; −$300 ends the run.
